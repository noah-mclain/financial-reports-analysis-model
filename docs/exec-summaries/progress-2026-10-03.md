## Can the remaining Arabic statements be gathered?

The owner asked on 3 October whether the remaining Arabic statements are impossible to gather, given the checkpoints they are needed for. **Not impossible in principle, but out of reach in the time left, and not needed to close week 1.** The "about 30 issuers" per stratum in [04-execution-phases.md](../blueprint/04-execution-phases.md) 2.4 is the size at which a stratum's own interval is judged; below it the result is reported as "not shown", which the plan allows. Reaching 30 would let that stratum be judged.

The arithmetic. Every row marked (est.) is an estimate and not a measurement; the others are counted from the files or the README.

| | Holdout (inside `train`) | `model_test` |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Issuers with Arabic documents now | 16 | 25 |
| Short of about 30 | 14 | 5 |
| Chance that a new issuer lands there (est.) | 0.65 x 0.15, about 10% | 20% |
| New clean Arabic issuers needed (est.) | about 140 | about 25 |
| Companies to screen at 69 kept in 317 screened, about 22% (est.) | about 660 | about 115 |
| Companies to screen at the main market's 36 in 203, about 18% (est.) | about 810 | about 140 |

The rates come from the README's latest re-screen: main market 36 of 203 companies kept, Nomu 33 of 114 (about 18% and 29%), so 69 of 317 together. They count companies with a clean Arabic edition kept, the rule that applies since 3 October. Both open Argaam listings are already screened. Earlier years sit behind the site's subscription and hold the same companies. Other Gulf exchanges list a few hundred companies each and each needs its own collector. So the holdout target is out of reach before 26 October. The `model_test` target may be reachable with one more market (no count of another market's companies has been made), and is an optional item if time allows.
