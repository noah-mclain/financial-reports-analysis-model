## The two items

| Item | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Spike: fuse an MLX LoRA adapter and run it as GGUF in llama.cpp | The route works. On a toy adapter over Qwen2.5-0.5B, the f16 GGUF gives the same answer as MLX on 10 of 10 held-out prompts. | Docker skeleton: base image, compose, both profiles start | Built. `make dev` and `make docker-up` each serve `/health` with their own profile name (`native`, `docker`). The image is 1.68 GB, builds cold in 148 s, and `docker compose up -d --wait` returns in 6.6 to 6.9 s. |

Agreement with MLX by quantization level (quantization stores the weights in fewer bits to shrink the file):

| Model | File | Same answer as MLX |
| --- | ---: | :---: |
| GGUF f16 | 960 MB | 10 / 10 |
| GGUF Q8_0 | 521 MB | 9 / 10 |
| GGUF Q6_K | 494 MB | 10 / 10 |
| GGUF Q5_K_M | 410 MB | 9 / 10 |
| GGUF Q4_K_M | 390 MB | 8 / 10 |

The plan's bar was 9 of 10. Q4_K_M misses it on two prompts, and those same two change at every level. In the f16 model they are near ties: the first and second choice are 0.3 and 0.6 points apart, against 4.6 points or more for the other eight. A near tie can flip under any rounding, so ten prompts cannot tell a tie from a real loss. The level has to be chosen on the real adapter.

"Same answer" is the measure, not "right answer". The toy adapter names the right id on only 2 of the 10 unseen labels.

## What this adds to week 2

- **OpenCV does not import in the image.** `import cv2` fails with `libxcb.so.1: cannot open shared object file`. The lock has `opencv-python`, not the headless build, and the slim base image lacks five system libraries. The OCR bake-off needs either those libraries or the headless wheel in the lock, and the image needs the Linux OCR engines anyway.
- **The quantization level is chosen by measurement on the real adapter,** in week 3: Q4_K_M against Q5_K_M on at least 50 held-out labels, recording the margin for every answer that differs.
- **Smaller items the image will meet in weeks 2 to 4:** the `app` user cannot write under `/app/var` until a volume is mounted for artifacts and the docling model cache; services have no memory limits; `llama.cpp:server` and `python:3.12-slim` are floating tags to pin by digest when the model service first runs; and there is no restart policy until the job queue arrives.

- Nothing in the spike ran in Docker. The CPU figures come from `-ngl 0` on macOS.
- Agreement was measured on English labels only. Arabic is untested.
- Route B (the base model as GGUF with the adapter loaded separately) was not tried, because route A agrees.
- The image was built on aarch64 only. x86-64 is not built.
- No OCR engine is in the image, and no model.
- The day's review was one read of the diff, as the plan set, not the six passes of week 1.
